# Press Kit License Template

This repository provides a free-to-use template for licensing press kit assets, with optional guidelines.

This template is released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0) (public domain). You may copy, modify, and use the template freely. The template is provided for informational purposes only and is not legal advice.

To apply the license to your press kit, please include the [license text](#press-kit-license) in your press kit instead of hotlinking this page.

## Guidelines
Welcome to the {TITLE} Press Kit! These assets are designed to help you talk about {TITLE}.
- Please do not stretch or distort the assets.
- Please do not use low-quality previews of the assets.
- Please do not remove logos or branding from the assets.
- Please do not hotlink the assets, because the URLs may change.

## Press Kit License
The assets in this press kit may be used to cover, discuss, or reference {TITLE} in articles, reviews, videos, and other media. You may resize, crop, or reformat assets as needed, but may not modify them in a way that is misleading or materially distorts the original material. All rights not expressly granted are reserved by {AUTHOR}.
